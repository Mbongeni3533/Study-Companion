# IELTS External Resources

Recommended free preparation channels, practice papers and apps, grouped by what
they are best at. Use one channel per skill rather than five at once.

## YouTube channels

- **FastTrack IELTS** - strong across all four skills, especially strategy.
  <https://youtube.com/c/FastrackEducation>

- **Crack IELTS with Rob** - listening and reading technique.
  <https://m.youtube.com/channel/UCrDeNIUi-e62DZnGq09KUuA>

- **English Speaking Success with Keith** - speaking, fluency and part 2 topics.
  <https://m.youtube.com/channel/UCiVm8XcbwS8-pcDEa5lFXIA>

- **IELTS Liz** - lessons, model answers and band descriptors.
  <https://ieltsliz.com>

- **Writing resource channel** - task 1 and task 2 structure and language.
  <https://m.youtube.com/channel/UCRSFsZwTpcm2IZKcWTOyyZg>

- **IELTS with Dave** - very good for writing, reading and speaking.
  <https://howtodoielts.com/blog/>

## How to use this list

1. Take one full Cambridge test under exam conditions to find your weakest skill.
2. Pick a single channel for that skill and follow it for two weeks.
3. Do one timed section every second day and review every wrong answer.
4. Book the test only when your timed scores are consistently at your target band.
